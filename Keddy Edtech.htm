FAQs

Got Questions? We've Got Answers

What Classes do you offer?

We offer online tutoring for IGCSE (Grades 6 to 10) and AS/A Levels (Grades 11 and 12), covering subjects like Physics, Chemistry, Maths, Biology, Accounts, and Computer Science.

How can I enroll in a classes?

You can enroll by visiting our website and selecting the desired course. For assistance, contact us at +91 8869945667 or email contact@keddy-edtech.org.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes, we offer a free 5-day demo class for new students to experience our teaching methods.

What is the duration of each class?

Each class is typically 1 hour long, with daily sessions totaling 3 to 4 hours.

How do you ensure quality teaching?

Our faculty consists of highly qualified professionals specializing in their respective subjects, ensuring effective and in-depth teaching.

What is your fee structure?

Our Single Subject Plan is priced at $50 per month, and the Multiple Subject Plan is $45 per month per subject. For customized plans, please contact us.

What is Keddy Ed-Tech’s online tutoring service?

Keddy Ed-Tech provides personalized, one-on-one online classes for IGCSE and A/AS Level students. Our expert tutors use interactive tools, live video lessons, and exam-focused strategies to help you master each subject.

What technology or equipment do I need?

You only need a stable internet connection, a laptop or tablet, and headphones. We use user-friendly online platforms that work on most devices.

Can I book a free trial class?

Absolutely. We offer a free demo class so you can experience our teaching style, interact with the tutor, and decide if it’s the right fit before enrolling.

Are your tutors qualified?

Yes. All Keddy Ed-Tech tutors are experienced educators who specialize in IGCSE & A/AS Level curricula. Many hold advanced degrees and have proven track records of improving student performance.

Which subjects do you teach?

We offer expert online tuition for core IGCSE & A/AS Level subjects such as Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, English, Business Studies, Economics, and Computer Science.

How do online tutoring sessions work?

Lessons are conducted live via secure video platforms. Tutors share presentations, whiteboards, and study materials in real time. Students can ask questions, solve problems, and get instant feedback — just like in-person tutoring.
